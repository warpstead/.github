### One canvas for everything you work with.

Warpstead is a spatial workspace for Windows 10 and 11. The programs, folders and files of a job sit side by side on one large canvas, instead of piling up in overlapping windows: zoom out to see the whole job, move to the part you need.

- **Programs on the canvas.** The applications you already use keep running as live windows on the canvas. Open one fullscreen when it needs all your attention, press Esc to come back.
- **A workspace for every job.** Each job gets its own named workspace, with its programs, folders and notes where you left them.
- **Folders that do the filing.** Links carry files from one step to the next (filter, classify, rename, move), with a preview before anything moves and undo afterwards.
- **Photos, start to finish.** Review and pick a shoot, adjust photos in place with a GPU preview, deliver finished images to a folder.
- **Instant search, offline.** Alt+Space finds files as you type in the folders you choose, by name, tags and metadata. It runs entirely on your PC.
- **AI that stays on your computer.** Images are tagged and described by a model that runs locally. Files are not uploaded anywhere for this.

**Try it in your browser:** [warpstead.com](https://warpstead.com) runs the real program, with a simulated Windows. Nothing to install.

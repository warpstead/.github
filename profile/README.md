### Your whole workflow, within reach.

Warpstead is a spatial workspace for Windows. The programs, folders and files you work with sit side by side on one canvas instead of being stacked in overlapping windows: zoom out to see your whole workflow, move to reach the part you need.

- **Programs on the canvas.** The applications you already use run as live windows, side by side.
- **Instant search.** Alt+Space finds files as you type, by name, tags, AI-written descriptions and metadata. Results open straight onto the canvas.
- **Linked folders and flows.** Links carry files from one step to the next (filter, classify, rename, move), with a preview before anything happens and undo afterwards.
- **Local AI classification.** Images are tagged and described by a model that runs on your computer. Files are not uploaded anywhere for this.
- **In-place editing.** Photos, PDFs and notes are edited right on the canvas, without opening another program.

Built for photographers, designers, video editors, developers, 3D artists, and anyone whose work lives in more than one window.

Warpstead is in development and not yet available to download. The preview runs in your browser from **7 October 2026**.
